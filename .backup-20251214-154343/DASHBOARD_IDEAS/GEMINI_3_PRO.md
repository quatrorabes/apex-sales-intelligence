***

## **1. Visual Hierarchy & Layout**

- **Header Bar**  
  - Use a *bold color gradient or soft shadow* for the header, with clear contrast between the user/contact name, role, and company.  
  - **Profile photo/initials:** Display a circle avatar—shows professionalism and helps with rapid scanning.

- **Tabbed Navigation**  
  - **Tabs** ("Overview", "Personal", "Company", "Personality", etc.) should stand out; use card-type or pill buttons, clear active/inactive states.  
  - Pin the tabs to the top as a sticky menu for long pages.

- **Section Cards/Containers**  
  - Implement *shadowed, rounded cards* for content modules (Overview, Background, Pain Points, Value Props).  
  - Use enough padding and margin to separate sections, preventing visual crowding.

***

## **2. Typography & Readability**

- **Font Choices**  
  - Apply a clean sans-serif font, such as Inter, Helvetica Neue, or Open Sans for all text.
  - **Hierarchy:**  
    - Names/Titles: Large (28–36px), bold  
    - Section headers: Medium (20–24px), semi-bold  
    - Body/Details: 16–18px, regular  
  - *Highlight keywords* (role, scores, value props) with color and font weight—use a subtle accent for data points.

- **Contrast & Accessibility**  
  - Ensure text color meets WCAG standards; dark backgrounds with light text for main boards.

***

## **3. Data & Scoring Visuals**

- **Score Cards (MDCP, RSS, Priority)**  
  - Use *compact cards with icons* and color cues (green for “Hot”, red for “Immediate”).
  - Consider *progress bars or circular meters*—add a small tooltip or legend for context.
  - Animated transitions when scores change enhance feedback.

- **Recommended Actions**  
  - Display call-to-action boxes with soft highlights and clear, actionable text (immediate, pending, etc.).
  - Add hover states and subtle icons for urgency.

***

## **4. Content Modules**

- **Overview/Background**  
  - Justify align bodies of text for consistency.  
  - Icons (e.g., info, briefcase, trophy) alongside section titles for visual quickness.

- **Pain Points / Value Propositions**  
  - Present bullets in *horizontal containers* or “chip” style, with icons (checkmarks, exclamation, etc.).
  - For lists: slightly alternate backgrounds for each row for reading comfort.

- **Products & Services, Leadership**  
  - Utilize *collapsible panels* or accordion sections for skimming or detailed reading.
  - Profile team headshots or initials for leadership modules.

***

## **5. Contact & Intelligence Modules**

- **Contact Info**  
  - Rowed cards (email, phone, company, title) with copy-to-clipboard icons; space elements out for tap-friendly UI.

- **AI Intelligence Status**  
  - “Enriched” badges, progress bars for data status, tooltip or expandable detail for last enrichment action.

- **Email Sequences/Content Generation**  
  - Stack email templates vertically with clear day markers.  
  - Use dashed borders or color blocks between sequences.  
  - Add “generate content” buttons with animations.

***

## **6. Table/List Views (Contacts Board)**

- **Tables**  
  - Sticky headers, sortable columns, alternating row highlight.
  - Action buttons (view, edit, enrich) at end of each row with consistent spacing.

- **Compact/Expanded View Toggle**  
  - Allow users to expand for more data or condense for scanning.

***

## **7. Personality Assessment Module**

- **Personality Type Highlight**  
  - Large, playful yet clean icon (brain, MBTI badge), short description.
  - Color-coded backgrounds based on type (e.g., purple for ENTJ).

- **Summary Card**  
  - Boxed module with a concise, readable explanation of what the type means within business context.

***

## **8. Responsive & Interactive Features**

- **Responsive Grid**  
  - Optimize for desktop/tablet/mobile—reflow cards, compress tables on small screens.
  - Sticky/floating buttons for high-priority actions (“Enrich”, “Generate Content”).

- **Microinteractions**  
  - Tooltips on hover/click for all critical data.  
  - Subtle click/press animations for critical controls.

***
