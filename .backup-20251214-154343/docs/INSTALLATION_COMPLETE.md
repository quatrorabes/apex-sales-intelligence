## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING

### Email not opening in client
**Fix:** Set your default mail app
- Mac: Mail app preferences
- Windows: Settings → Apps → Default apps → Email
- Or manually copy/paste email content

### Call script modal not showing
**Fix:** Enable popups in browser
- Click the popup blocker icon
- Allow popups from localhost
- Refresh page

### Contact page shows "Not enriched"
**Fix:** Enrich the contact first
- Go back to main dashboard
- Find contact in table
- Click "Enrich" button
- Wait 2-3 minutes
- Refresh contact page

### LinkedIn not opening
**Fix:** Check LinkedIn URL in database
- Some contacts may not have LinkedIn
- URL format: linkedin.com/in/username
- Manually add if missing

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### Email Tips:
1. **Best times to send:**
   - Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm
   - Avoid Mondays and Fridays
   - Never weekends (B2B)

2. **Subject line hacks:**
   - Keep under 50 characters
   - Use their company name
   - Ask questions
   - Create curiosity

3. **Body optimization:**
   - Under 100 words for Email 1
   - One clear CTA
   - Easy to reply (yes/no question)

### Call Tips:
1. **Warm up first:**
   - Send email
   - Wait 5 minutes
   - Call: "I just sent you an email..."

2. **Use scripts but don't read:**
   - Internalize key points
   - Sound natural
   - Adapt to conversation

3. **Always have next step:**
   - Book specific time
   - Not "sometime next week"
   - Send calendar invite immediately

### LinkedIn Tips:
1. **Profile before connect:**
   - View their profile first
   - Comment on recent post
   - Then send connection

2. **Custom notes win:**
   - 90% accept rate with note
   - 40% without
   - Reference mutual connection if possible

3. **Post-connection:**
   - Don't pitch immediately
   - Engage with content first
   - Build rapport over 2-3 days
