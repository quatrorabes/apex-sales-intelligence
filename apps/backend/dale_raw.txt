- **Business model:**  
  - **Privately held commercial real estate finance and investment company**, with a focus on **multifamily and healthcare lending**, **loan servicing**, and **investment management**.[2]

- **Mission & value proposition:**  
  - Mission articulated by founder Stephen Rosenberg: Greystone exists **“to help people”**, with cultural standards of **integrity, excellence, entrepreneurship, and caring**.[1]
