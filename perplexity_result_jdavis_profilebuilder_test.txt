### Overview
Jennifer Davis is the Senior Vice President of Business Development at TMC Financing, a nonprofit Certified Development Company specializing in SBA 504 commercial real estate loans. She is based in Southern California and has over 35 years of experience in financial services, with a focus on empowering small business growth and economic development in Orange County, the Inland Empire, Los Angeles, and San Diego.[1][2][3]

### Background
Davis joined TMC Financing in 2012 and has led the Southern California office, significantly increasing the company’s market share. Prior to TMC, she held senior roles at Bank of America, including senior vice president and market manager. Under her leadership, TMC Financing has facilitated over $1.7 billion in commercial real estate financing for more than 500 small businesses, resulting in the creation or retention of approximately 2,000 jobs.[2][3][1]
