# Greg Richter: Chief Executive Officer at Medalist Partners

## Current Role and Responsibilities

Greg Richter serves as **Chief Executive Officer and Partner at Medalist Partners**, a $2 billion alternative credit manager headquartered in New York that specializes in asset-based private credit.[1][2] In addition to his CEO duties, Richter holds the position of **Co-Head of Structured Credit and Asset Finance** and serves as a **Co-Portfolio Manager** for the funds managed by Medalist.[2][3] He assumed these roles when he co-founded Medalist Partners on May 1, 2018, alongside Michael Ardisson and John Slonieski.[5]

## Career History

Richter's career in finance spans nearly four decades, beginning in 1985 during the formative years of the structured credit market.[4] His professional trajectory demonstrates deep expertise in asset-based finance and securitized products:

**Prudential Securities (approximately 1985-2000)**
Richter began his career at Prudential Securities in a middle-office role that bridged operations and the mortgage trading desk.[4] This early exposure provided him with foundational knowledge in mortgage funding, allocations, and trading. He progressed rapidly through the organization, eventually becoming a **Managing Director** and **Head of Trading and Syndicate for all structured products**.[2] During his tenure at Prudential, which lasted approximately 15 years, Richter led all structured credit trading, including CMBS, RMBS, ABS, and CDOs.[4] His team achieved a significant milestone by creating and selling the first ABS CDOs, which subsequently became a major market segment.[4]

**Credit Suisse (2000-2008)**
Richter joined Credit Suisse in 2000, where he initially oversaw **trading and syndication for securitized products**.[4] Under his leadership, the firm packaged and sold $100-200 billion of bonds annually.[4] His responsibilities expanded significantly over his tenure. He became **Head of Credit Suisse's Specialty Finance Group**, based in New York, where he was responsible for the combined Global Asset Finance Capital Markets and Specialty Finance Banking Groups.[2] He also served as a **member of Credit Suisse's Fixed Income Operating Committee**.[2] Additionally, Richter headed the **Asset Backed Securities Home Equity (ABSHE) shelf**, which bought and packaged mortgage loans.[2] His role encompassed oversight of all securitization activity in the U.S., Europe, Australia, and Emerging Markets.[2]

**Candlewood Investment Group (2008-2018)**
Following the 2008 financial crisis, Richter transitioned to asset management. He co-founded and led the **Structured Credit and Asset Finance business** within Candlewood Investment Group, LP, serving as a Partner and Director.[2]

**Medalist Partners (2018-Present)**
Richter co-founded Medalist Partners in May 2018, where he has served as CEO and Partner for over seven years.[5] The firm was established to provide funding in specialty finance, particularly where traditional banks have retreated.[4]

## Education and Credentials

Richter holds a **Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics from Colgate University**.[2]

## Professional Achievements and Recognition

Richter's career has been marked by several significant accomplishments:

- **Pioneer in ABS CDO Market**: His team at Prudential created and sold the first ABS CDOs, establishing a market that grew substantially in subsequent years.[4]
- **Large-Scale Securitization Operations**: At Credit Suisse, he oversaw the packaging and sale of $100-200 billion in bonds annually across multiple markets and product types.[4]
- **2025 Icon Recognition**: Richter was recognized as a 2025 Icon by ABF Journal, acknowledging his nearly four decades at the center of innovation in asset-based finance.[4]
- **Firm Leadership**: He successfully established Medalist Partners as a $2 billion alternative credit manager focused on asset-based private credit, managing capital for institutional investors including pensions, endowments, foundations, family offices, and high-net-worth individuals.[1][5]

## Speaking Engagements and Media Appearances

Richter has been active in the speaking circuit. He has appeared as a speaker and organizer at industry conferences, including events hosted by the Council of Federal Advisors of New York (CFANY).[1] His media presence includes recognition in industry publications such as ABF Journal, where he has shared insights on the evolution of structured credit markets and the specialty finance sector.[4]

## Professional Associations and Board Positions

In addition to his CEO role at Medalist Partners, Richter serves as a **Director/Board Member** at Revolution Microelectronics (America), Inc., a manufacturer and distributor of aerospace-grade horticultural lighting and controls products headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.[5]

## Professional Philosophy

Richter emphasizes leadership through example and interpersonal relationships. He has stated: "Always treat people with respect and thoughtfulness. This is a people business. Never underestimate the importance of a handshake and good conversation."[4] His vision for Medalist Partners is to establish it as a trusted, innovative firm that addresses market needs in specialty finance where traditional banking has contracted.[4]
