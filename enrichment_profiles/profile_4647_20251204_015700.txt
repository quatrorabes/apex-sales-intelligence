Greg Richter is the Chief Executive Officer and Partner at Medalist Partners, a prominent alternative credit manager based in New York. With nearly four decades of experience in finance, Richter has been at the forefront of structured credit and asset finance, demonstrating a deep expertise in asset-based finance and securitized products. His leadership at Medalist Partners involves strategic direction, portfolio management, and oversight of operations focused on structured and asset-based credit.

# 2. PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

## 2.1 Current Role & Responsibilities
Greg Richter serves as the Chief Executive Officer and Partner at Medalist Partners. He is Co-Head of Structured Credit and Asset Finance and Co-Portfolio Manager for the funds managed by the firm. His responsibilities include setting the strategic direction of the firm, managing portfolios, and overseeing alternative asset management operations focused on structured and asset-based credit.

## 2.2 Career Trajectory
Richter's career began in 1985 at Prudential Securities, where he advanced to Managing Director and head of Trading and Syndicate for all structured products. He then joined Credit Suisse in 2000, eventually becoming Global Head of the Specialty Finance Group. Following the 2008 financial crisis, he co-founded the Structured Credit and Asset Finance business within Candlewood Investment Group. He later co-founded Medalist Partners, where he currently serves as CEO.

## 2.3 Education & Credentials
Richter holds a B.A. in Economics from Colgate University.

## 2.4 Core Competencies
- Strategic Leadership
- Portfolio Management
- Structured Credit Expertise
- Asset-Based Finance
- Regulatory Compliance
- Relationship Building
