### **Los Angeles Regional SBDC Network – Business Research Overview**

---

#### **1. Overview**

The **Los Angeles Regional Small Business Development Center (LA SBDC) Network** is a federally and state-funded small business assistance program serving entrepreneurs and small businesses across Los Angeles, Santa Barbara, and Ventura counties. It is part of the national **America’s SBDC** network, the largest small business economic development program in the United States, administered by the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA).

The LA SBDC Network provides **no-cost, confidential, one-on-one business advising**, low-cost training, and access to capital and market expansion resources. It operates as a regional network of service centers and satellite locations, hosted by community colleges and nonprofit organizations, with Long Beach City College serving as the lead host institution.

The network is among the top 10 SBDC networks in the U.S. by funding and reach, having supported over **50,000 small business owners** in starting, growing, and sustaining their businesses.

---

#### **2. Founding & History**

- The **national SBDC program** began as a pilot in 1976, with California State Polytechnic University, Pomona among the first centers.
- The **California SBDC Network** was re-established in 1991, ensuring every state had an SBDC program.
- The **Los Angeles Regional SBDC Network** was formally structured as a regional network under the California SBDC system, with **Long Beach City College (LBCC)** becoming the **lead host institution** on **January 1, 2006**.
- Since then, LBCC has held the SBA contract and coordinates a network of service centers across the three-county region.
