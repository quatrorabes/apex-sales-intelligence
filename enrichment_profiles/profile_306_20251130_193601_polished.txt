## 2. Professional Background
Moritz joined Newmark in 2017, bringing with him a substantial track record in commercial real estate finance. Before Newmark, he spent eight years with Regency Capital Partners, a San Francisco-based boutique real estate investment bank, where he was involved in the placement of over $5 billion in debt and equity. Prior to that, he worked in the Real Estate Group of Capital Source in San Francisco and started his career as an appraiser for the Property Sciences Group in Concord, California.
